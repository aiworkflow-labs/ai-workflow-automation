# CRM ChatGPT Integration

## Overview

This workflow connects CRM systems with ChatGPT for automated responses, lead qualification, and customer interaction.

---

## Workflow Steps

1. Receive CRM lead data
2. Send customer inquiry to ChatGPT
3. Generate AI response
4. Store AI output in CRM
5. Trigger follow-up automation
6. Notify sales team

---

## Tools Used

- OpenAI API
- CRM APIs
- Zapier
- Webhooks
- Python

---

## Business Outcome

- Faster response times
- Automated lead engagement
- Improved customer support
- Reduced manual work
